Pulse Support

Need help with Pulse? We're here to help with your account, learning experience, notifications, widgets, and subscriptions.

Contact us

Email chris.ader.contact@gmail.com with your question or a description of the problem.

For technical issues, it helps to include the app version, your device model, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords, sign-in links, or payment card details.

You can also send feedback or report a problem from Pulse's Settings screen.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed through your Apple account. You can view or cancel App Store subscriptions. If you're having trouble with a purchase or restoring access, contact us at the email address above.

Account deletion

To delete your Pulse account, use Settings → Delete Account in the app, or follow the account-deletion instructions in our Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription.
