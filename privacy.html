Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulse, a mobile learning app operated by Recall Labs Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Pulse”, “we”, “us”), processes information when you use our apps, websites, and related services. We act as the data controller for the personal data we determine how and why to process.

1. How to contact us

For questions about this policy, or to request access to or deletion of your data, contact chris.ader.contact@gmail.com. You can also request account deletion directly in the app under Settings.

2. Information Pulse processes

  • Accounts: account identifiers, email addresses, authentication information, and profile information that you provide. Authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider.
  • Learning activity: languages and playlists you select, learning preferences, responses, activity, and progress.
  • Search and feature use: information about searches and feature requests you make, and related app usage.
  • Your learning material: text or phrases you save, enter, or send to Pulse through sharing features, plus any associated translations and learning records.
  • Devices and notifications: device and app information, language and region settings, network characteristics, and push notification tokens. We use this information to operate, protect and improve Pulse.
  • Network and location information: IP addresses, approximate country-level location derived from network connections, and related access times. This information may be associated with your account. We do not collect precise GPS location for this purpose.
  • Subscriptions: subscription tier and entitlement status, product IDs, purchase and transaction information, and identifiers needed to reconcile entitlements. We do not receive your complete payment card number from Apple or Google.
  • Help and reports: feedback, content reports, messages, optional contact details, and relevant troubleshooting context you submit. Limited security and service logs may also be processed.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing Pulse (contract): creating and managing accounts, providing learning features, storing learning material and preferences, delivering notifications, maintaining learning progress, and supplying paid benefits.
  • Billing and recordkeeping (contract and legal obligation): verifying purchases, handling restores and renewals, processing support requests and complying with applicable financial and consumer laws.
  • Security and service reliability (legitimate interests): helping protect accounts and our services, detecting or investigating possible abuse, fraud or unauthorized activity, troubleshooting problems, and maintaining service reliability and availability, while respecting applicable objection rights.
  • Service analytics and improvement (legitimate interests): We may also use technical and network information for service analytics and to improve Pulse.
  • Optional permissions (consent or user choice, where required): sending device notifications and accessing device features that require operating-system permissions. You can adjust these permissions in your device settings.
  • Responding to requests and legal obligations: addressing privacy requests, enforcing rights and complying with applicable law.

Some information is necessary to provide an account-based learning service; without it, parts of Pulse cannot function. We do not use your saved learning content for third-party advertising.

4. Who processes information for Pulse

  • Supabase: authentication, database, backend functions and storing account, learning, feedback and related service records. Our Supabase project is hosted in Paris, France. Supabase privacy information.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement management. Pulse identifies your subscription record to RevenueCat using an app user ID and, when available, sends an account email and display name; RevenueCat also processes purchase-related information. RevenueCat privacy information.
  • Expo: software-update delivery and push-notification routing. When a notification is sent through Expo, the push token and notification payload (which may include learning text) are transmitted for delivery to the device through Apple or Google platform services.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution, in-app purchase processing, and device notification delivery where applicable.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this public legal website. GitHub may receive information ordinarily associated with visits to a web page; see GitHub's privacy statement.

We may also provide information to authorities when required by law. We seek appropriate safeguards from service providers that process personal information on our behalf.

5. International processing

Pulse’s primary Supabase project is hosted in Paris, France. Other providers and their infrastructure may process information in additional countries, including the United States. Where legally required, international transfers are subject to applicable data-protection safeguards, such as contractual protections or a valid adequacy mechanism. Contact us for more information about safeguards that apply to a particular transfer.

6. Retention and account deletion

Pulse, operated by Recall Labs Inc., allows you to request permanent deletion of your Pulse account and associated learning data in either of these ways:

  • Within Pulse: Open Settings → Delete Account and complete the confirmation process.
  • Without the app: Email chris.ader.contact@gmail.com with the subject Pulse account deletion request. If you can, send it from your account email; otherwise, explain that you need an alternative way to verify your identity. You do not need to reinstall Pulse. Please do not email your password, login codes, or payment details. We may request reasonable verification before acting and will confirm once the request is processed.

Account deletion removes your Pulse account and associated account-owned learning records from active application systems, including your saved learning material, playlists, responses, and learning progress. Limited records may remain in backups, service logs, or third-party purchase systems for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or other legal requirements. We keep those records only as long as necessary for those purposes under applicable retention policies.

We generally retain account-linked technical and security information, including network information, while your account remains active. Account deletion removes this information from active account records, subject to the limited backup, service-log and legal-retention exceptions described above.

Deleting your Pulse account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel recurring billing separately through your Apple App Store or Google Play account. You may request account deletion even if your subscription has not yet been canceled.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of, or a copy of your information; ask us to restrict certain processing; object to processing based on legitimate interests; or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Contact us using the address in section 1. Residents of the EEA may lodge a complaint with their competent data-protection authority.

8. Children

Pulse is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without authorization required by applicable law, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page and provide additional notice if required by law.
